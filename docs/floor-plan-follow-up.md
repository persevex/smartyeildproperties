## What changed

- Added drawings from official project microsites, dedicated plan PDFs and original diagrams within project brochures.
- Recovered older scanned plans, correctly rendered small-format PDFs and reviewed diagrams later in long brochures.
- Kept all published assets local, with source URLs, page/image references and integrity hashes recorded internally in `floor-plan-sources.json`.
- Preserved actual project identity and the original plan dimensions. Did not redraw plans, invent layouts, remove image watermarks or reuse another project's plan.
- Excluded covers, photos, decorative diagrams, corrupted image fragments, listing-site watermarks and plans without a reliable match. The three MARQ phases were not populated indiscriminately with the same layouts; Tower 6/7 plans are labelled explicitly for the applicable project listing.
- No new public source links or competing-site branding were added.
